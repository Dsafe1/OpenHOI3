# The icon

`icon.png` and `icon.ico` at the repository's root - the exe's icon, and the window's and the taskbar's - are made by
`make_icon.py` from `battleships.webp`: the leading ship of a line of battleships, bow on, cut square and coloured as at
sunset (an orange sky, a copper sea, the ships dark against them), with rounded corners. Made again with

    python tools/icon/make_icon.py .

from the repository's root. The colouring is the script's own, by hand: the sky, the sea and the ships each given a hue,
the photograph's light and shade kept.

## The photograph

`battleships.webp`, 800 x 641, black and white: a column of battleships under way, the leading one bow on, smoke from the
ships behind. **Public domain**, as the maintainer found it on 2026-11-13. **Its source is still to be written here** - the
page it came from and the licence that page gives - so that the claim can be checked.

It is not taken from an install, so the rule that nothing of an install's is committed does not touch it. The maintainer
has also made the icon the one exception to that rule (2026-11-13), should it ever be made from a loading screen instead;
nothing else is excepted. The loading screen of a tank is a still from the film *Fury* (2014) and is not to be used.
